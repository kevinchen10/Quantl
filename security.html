Security policy

Effective November 12, 2026

Found something that looks like a security problem? Thank you. Please email [CONTACT EMAIL] with the details, and don't make it public until we've had a chance to fix it.

How to report

  • Email [CONTACT EMAIL] with “Security” in the subject. Include what you found, where you found it, and the steps to reproduce it.
  • We aim to reply within 5 business days, and to keep you updated until it's fixed.
  • Please give us a reasonable time to fix the problem (normally 90 days) before you disclose it publicly.

Please do

  • Test only against your own browser session.
  • Use the smallest amount of testing needed to show the problem.
  • Stop and tell us as soon as you confirm an issue.

Please don't

  • attack availability (no denial-of-service, flooding or load testing);
  • use automated scanners that send large amounts of traffic;
  • access, change or delete data that isn't yours;
  • social-engineer or phish anyone;
  • attack our hosting provider's infrastructure, which has its own programs;
  • use a weakness for any purpose other than reporting it to us.

Safe harbor

If you make a good-faith effort to follow this policy, we will consider your research authorized, will not pursue or support legal action against you for it, and will work with you to understand and fix the issue. This safe harbor covers only our own systems and claims we control. It doesn't cover your rights or obligations to anyone else.

Out of scope

  • Changing your own scores or progress in your own browser. They are stored on your device and affect only you. (Using that to fool other players, for example by faking shared results or counts, is still against the Terms.)
  • Missing best-practice headers or settings with no demonstrated impact.
  • Reports from automated tools without a working example.

Machine-readable contact details are at /.well-known/security.txt.
