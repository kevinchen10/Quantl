Step 1 of 6 · animation primitives & style guide

1Palette

Eight colours on near-black. Each one has a single job in every round, so the visual language only has to be learned once. Blue and teal look alike, so they never appear side by side as a contrast.

2Type

3Motion

  • Motion explains the math. If a movement doesn’t carry meaning, it doesn’t happen.
  • Easing is smooth ease-in-out, usually 0.4–1.2 s. No bounce, no springs, no shadows, no gradients.
  • Groups ripple in with a lagged start. They never pop in all at once.
  • Every round ends in a reveal: truth beside guess, the score ticks up, then a skippable 5–12 s “why”.

4Primitives

Each card is a live scene built from the reusable primitives in src/anim. Scenes play when scrolled into view. Skip jumps to the end, the same mechanism that makes each round’s explanation skippable.

Everything on this page is code: Bézier paths, KaTeX glyph outlines and a small timeline library. Nothing is drawn by hand or pre-rendered. The game itself is index.html.
