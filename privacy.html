Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

The short version. QuantL has no accounts, no ads, no tracking cookies and no analytics.

Your progress lives in your own browser, and the game doesn't send it to us. Our hosting provider sees the basic technical information every website gets, and uses it to deliver and protect the site.

1. Who we are

QuantL is run by Kevin Chen (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

2. What is stored in your browser

To let you pick up where you left off, QuantL saves a few things in your browser's local storage on your own device:

  • today's progress: which rounds you've played, your answers, your in-progress answer and the round clock;
  • your scores and results;
  • settings, such as whether sound is muted and your Quick Math practice ranges;
  • answer counts that QuantL uses on your device to estimate how rare an answer is.

The game doesn't send this information to us. It stays on your device until you clear it. You can do that any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings, which also resets your progress.

3. What our hosting provider processes

QuantL is delivered by a hosting and network-security provider, currently Cloudflare. As with any website, delivering pages to you means processing technical information such as:

  • your IP address;
  • your browser and device type;
  • the pages requested;
  • the time of each request.

This information is used to serve the site, keep it fast and protect it from abuse and attacks. It is handled under the provider's own privacy terms. We don't use it to build profiles of players.

4. What we don't do

  • We don't ask for your name, email or any account details to play. (The optional feedback link above is the only place you might give them, and only if you choose to.)
  • We don't use advertising, tracking cookies, third-party analytics or social-media trackers.
  • We don't sell or rent personal information.
  • Your shared result string contains only bars and scores, never your answers.

5. Feedback and question ideas (optional)

The game may show a small link, "Got a question idea? Tell us", that opens a feedback form or an email to us in a new tab. The game itself sends nothing: the link carries only the puzzle number and the page it came from. Whatever you write, and any email address you choose to give, reaches us only if you send it. We use it to read your feedback, to reply if you ask us to, and, only if you tick the box allowing it, to credit you by the name you give when we use your idea. If the link opens a form run by another company, that company's privacy policy also applies to what you type there. To have your feedback deleted, contact us.

6. If this changes

We may later add features that need a server, such as counting how many players found the same answer or showing real score distributions. If we do, we plan to send only what the feature needs: an anonymous summary of your answer and score, not your identity. We will update this policy before any such change takes effect.

7. Children

QuantL is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in Europe or state privacy laws such as California's), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of personal information about you. Since QuantL keeps your game data on your own device, you control it directly. For anything else, contact us and we will help.

9. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect QuantL, including HTTPS everywhere and strict browser security headers. No system is perfectly secure. If you find a problem, please tell us through our security page.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. For important changes, we will also show a notice in the game.
